The Best Thing I Read Lately

The best thing I read recently was an article about two people who built an app as a side hustle while working full-time and sold it for almost $4 million.

I was amazed that they did not need to quit their jobs or take significant risks at first. They developed the product on the side, solved a simple problem, and continuously improved it until it was valuable enough to sell.

I liked the article because it proved that successful businesses can start small. Identifying and solving a real world problem influences my perspective on side projects and software ideas.
